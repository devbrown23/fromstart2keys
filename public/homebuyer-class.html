Questions

Good to know.

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no cost and no obligation to work with us.

Do I need to attend both nights?

Yes. To receive your Washington homebuyer education certificate, plan to attend both evenings in full with your camera on so attendance can be confirmed.

What if I'm not ready to buy yet?

That's the best time to come. Most attendees are 6–24 months out, and the certificate is good for two years.

Can my partner or co-buyer join?

Yes, and we recommend it. Each person who wants a certificate should register under their own name and email.

Do I need perfect credit or 20% down?

No. We'll walk through programs with low down payments and assistance options, and what credit range each one looks for.

How do I get the Zoom link?

Register on Eventbrite and your link is emailed to you, with reminders before each night.
