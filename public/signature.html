Put it in Gmail

  1. Tap Copy signature above.
  2. In Gmail, click the ⚙️ gear → See all settings.
  3. On the General tab, scroll to Signature. Select your old signature, delete it, and paste (Ctrl + V).
  4. Under Signature defaults, pick it for new emails and replies.
  5. Scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes. Then send yourself a test email.

On the Gmail phone app: it can only add a plain-text signature. In the app's Settings, turn Mobile signature off so it doesn't stack a second one under your emails.
